The Brew Haven Process

How we bring premium, ethically sourced coffees from high-altitude slopes straight to your cup.

Step 01

Ethical Direct Sourcing

We travel directly to equatorial micro-lots in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra. By purchasing directly from farmer cooperatives, we pay 35% above fair-trade thresholds to foster direct, generational sustainability.

Step 02

Micro-Batch Roasting

Every green bean batch is roasted in our custom fluid-bed roaster in batches under 15 lbs. We monitor convective airflow and heat margins precisely to develop fruit notes and round sugars without roasting char.

Step 03

Sensory Profiling

Our certified sensory baristas sample and cup each roast batch daily. We calibrate extraction ratios, dissolved solids, and taste logs to verify flavor notes like orange citrus, hazelnut caramel, and stone fruit.

Step 04

The Perfect Extraction

Whether choosing a rich 9bar double espresso pull, an 18-hour cold brew draft, or a custom slow hand-pour V60, our water chemistry and grind scales are structured to yield the perfect cup.

Our Journey & Philosophy

Founded in 2021, Brew Haven began as a small roasting project in a vintage brick warehouse. Our vision was simple: to create a sanctuary where masterfully crafted coffee acts as a catalyst for creative community and cozy rest.

We build deep relationships with our farmers across Central America and East Africa, paying well above fair-trade standards to support sustainable agricultural communities. Every bean is roasted in our custom fluid-bed roaster, monitored precisely by our certified roasters.
